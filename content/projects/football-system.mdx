Teams register, fixtures generate, matches simulate, the table updates. Put like
that it sounds small, but the interesting part is that every one of those steps
has to be reversible — someone always registers a team wrong.

## Structure

The engine keeps the tournament and the simulation apart. The tournament knows
about rounds, fixtures and standings; the simulation knows about a single match
and nothing else. Keeping that line clean meant the scoring rules could change
without touching the bracket logic.
