The first version of this was two pages of a sketchbook: a week laid out in
columns, each day a stack of things I might do, most of them crossed out by the
time I actually left.

## What it does

You add destinations, give each one a rough duration, and the planner lays them
out across the days you have. Everything is editable in place — the point is to
keep rearranging until the shape looks right, the same way the paper version
worked.

## What I'd change

The data model assumes a trip is a flat list of days. Real trips nest: a week in
Finland contains three days in Helsinki that contain an afternoon somewhere. The
next version should let a day contain a plan of its own.
