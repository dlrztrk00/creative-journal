No engine, no sprite library, no physics package. GLUT gives you a window and a
render loop, and everything after that is yours: the ships, the collision
checks, the timing, the score.

## What I learned

That a game loop is a discipline. Every frame you get one pass to read input,
advance the world, and draw it — and any state you forget to advance is a bug
that only shows up when the frame rate changes.

## Notes

The satisfying part was the collision code. It is a few lines of arithmetic, and
getting it right is the difference between a game and a screensaver.
